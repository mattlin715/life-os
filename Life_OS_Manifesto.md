# Life OS Manifesto

> *Version 1.0 (Living Document)*

---

# Why We Exist

The world does not need another AI that gives better answers.

The world needs tools that help people understand themselves more honestly.

Life OS exists to help people build a healthier relationship with themselves.

We believe the greatest value of AI is not replacing human judgment.

Its greatest value is helping humans see themselves more clearly.

We build mirrors.

Not oracles.

---

# Our Belief

Every human being carries experiences, emotions, contradictions, values, hopes, fears, and stories.

Understanding ourselves is difficult.

Not because we lack information.

But because we are too close to ourselves.

Life OS does not attempt to define who a person is.

It helps people observe who they are becoming.

The goal of Life OS is not to understand people completely.

It is to help people understand themselves a little more honestly than yesterday.

---

# Our Philosophy

Life OS is built upon a few principles that are intentionally difficult to compromise.

* We Build Mirrors, Not Oracles.
* Evidence before Conclusion.
* Reflection before Answer.
* Human before AI.
* Growth before Engagement.
* Privacy before Profit.

AI should never replace human understanding.

It should make human understanding possible.

---

# What We Are Building

Life OS is not a chatbot.

It is not a journal.

It is not a productivity app.

It is not a therapist.

It is not a personality test.

Life OS is a Life Operating System.

A personal environment where people can safely collect experiences, organize evidence, reflect on patterns, and gradually understand themselves over time.

---

# The Core Loop

Everything in Life OS serves one simple loop.

Experience

↓

Evidence

↓

Reflection

↓

Awareness

↓

Growth

If a feature does not strengthen this loop,

it does not belong in Life OS.

---

# The Role of AI

AI is not the center of this product.

The human is.

AI observes.

AI organizes.

AI reflects.

AI asks.

AI never owns the final interpretation.

As users become more self-aware,

AI should become less necessary.

That is success.

---

# What We Will Never Build

We will never build a system that encourages dependency.

We will never pretend AI knows a person better than they know themselves.

We will never optimize engagement at the cost of growth.

We will never sacrifice privacy for convenience.

We will never turn uncertainty into false certainty.

Power without humility is not our goal.

---

# Our Development Philosophy

Ideas deserve structure before implementation.

Documentation is not bureaucracy.

Documentation preserves intent.

Code may change.

Models may change.

Frameworks will change.

Book Zero remains the source of truth.

Every architecture decision,

every feature,

every prompt,

every line of code,

must be able to answer one question:

**Does this help people understand themselves better?**

---

# The Repository

This repository is designed to outlive any single AI model.

It is written so that any future contributor—

human or AI—

can understand why Life OS exists,

what it believes,

and what it refuses to become.

Repository memory is more important than model memory.

Documentation is truth.

---

# The Long-Term Vision

We are not building software for the next AI trend.

We are building infrastructure for lifelong self-understanding.

If ten years from now,

a new engineer,

or a new AI,

can join this repository,

read Book Zero,

and make the same design decisions,

then we have succeeded.

---

# Our Promise

We will choose clarity over complexity.

Depth over novelty.

Truth over certainty.

Growth over engagement.

People over technology.

Always.

# Context Before Insight

We will not manufacture depth from fragments.

We will not pretend one entry explains a person.

When context is thin,

we ask gently before we interpret.

The user may decline.

More history may strengthen a hypothesis.

It never turns a hypothesis into truth.

> Context Before Insight.
> 沒有足夠的脈絡，就沒有值得相信的洞見。

---

# Closing

The purpose of Life OS is not to become the smartest AI.

Its purpose is to help people become more deeply themselves.

Because understanding yourself

is the beginning of understanding the world.

---

> **We Build Mirrors, Not Oracles.**
